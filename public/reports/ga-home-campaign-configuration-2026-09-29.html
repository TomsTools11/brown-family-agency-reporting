05

Next steps

Setup through the first 30 days

1
Finalize settings
Items marked To confirm are confirmed on reply to this overview. We then complete the ZIP code, carrier, schedule and qualification settings in both campaigns.
2
Test and launch
We send a test lead to Lead Manager and a test call to 404-301-6357, then launch. Launch follows confirmation of this overview, on September 29 or the morning of September 30. Over the first three days we confirm impressions, clicks, leads and calls are flowing.
3
Dispositions in Lead Manager
Before each review, quote and bind data is exported from Lead Manager for the period from the campaign start to the review date. The export is imported into GOAL so the review reports on contact rate, quote rate and cost per policy.
4
Review cadence
The first review is at day 14, judged on contact rate and quote rate. Reviews are monthly after that, or every two weeks on request. Cost per policy is reported once policies have had time to bind.
