06

Recommendations

Proposed changes to the GA Home ZIP code list

1
Add the 5 High-Share ZIP Codes Inside the Targeted Counties
30269 Peachtree City, 30265 Newnan, 30277 Sharpsburg, 30276 Senoia, 30290 Tyrone. Together: 26,761 homes in range at a 67.2% share, all within 25 miles of the office. With these added, the list covers 138,282 in-range homes at a 43.1% share (52 ZIP codes).
2
Review the 15 ZIP Codes Under 25% in Range
8,943 of 55,253 owner-occupied homes in these ZIP codes (16.2%) are valued $300K to $750K; 14 of the 15 have a typical value below $300K. The home-value range is applied only through the ZIP code list. Removing them and adding the 5 ZIP codes above gives a 37-ZIP list covering 129,339 in-range homes at a 48.7% share.
3
Confirm the Three Fulton ZIP Codes
30327 Atlanta: typical value $1.56M, 18% of homes in range; 30344 East Point: typical value $226K, 40% of homes in range; 30350 Sandy Springs: typical value $527K, 30% of homes in range. They are 22 to 41 miles from the office.
4
Apply the Property-Age Rule as a Campaign Filter, Not Through the ZIP List
The share of owner-occupied homes built 1990 or later ranges from 30248 Locust Grove 90%, 30253 McDonough 89%, 30228 Hampton 86% down to 30297 Forest Park 10%, 30344 East Point 20%, 30350 Sandy Springs 26% across the current list. The property-age filter (30 or 40 years, to confirm) is set on the campaign and applies to every ZIP code.
5
Hold the Adjacent-County Candidates for the day-30 Review
30213 Fairburn in south Fulton; Rockdale (30094, 30013); Newton (30014) meet the 40% share and 1,500-home thresholds. We review them against lead volume and contact rate by ZIP code after the first 30 days.
6
Check Delivery by ZIP Code After Launch
Home counts describe the housing stock, not ad inventory. At the day-14 source review we compare impressions and leads by ZIP code against this list and adjust where a ZIP code with many in-range homes is receiving little traffic.
07

Method and Sources

Definitions, thresholds and data files

  • Home values. American Community Survey 2020-2024 5-year estimates, table B25075 (value of owner-occupied housing units). The range used is $300,000 to $749,999 because ACS brackets are $300K-$400K, $400K-$500K and $500K-$750K. At a $700K ceiling, assuming an even distribution inside the $500K-$750K bracket, the statewide in-range count is 1,079,336 instead of 1,145,516 (-5.8%); rankings do not change.
  • Other ACS fields. B25077 median value, B25003 tenure (owner-occupied share), B25036 owner-occupied homes by year built. Values are owner-reported and pooled over five survey years; margins of error are wider in ZIP codes with few homes.
  • Typical value. Zillow Home Value Index by ZIP code, August 2026 (all homes, mid-tier, smoothed, seasonally adjusted). In range means $300,000 to $700,000. All 47 target ZIP codes have a Zillow value.
  • Geography. ZIP codes are Census ZIP Code Tabulation Areas (ZCTAs), which approximate USPS ZIP codes. Each ZCTA is assigned to the county holding most of its land area (Census 2020 relationship file). City names follow USPS. Distances are straight-line from 1074 Bear Creek Blvd, Hampton, to the area centroid.
  • Scoring. ZIP codes with fewer than 200 owner-occupied homes are not scored. Share in range = homes $300K to $750K divided by owner-occupied homes. Thresholds in section 05: additions require a 60% share, 1,500 homes in range, a typical value in $300K to $700K and a location within 30 miles of the office inside a targeted county; further candidates use a 40% share with the same other conditions. Low-share ZIP codes in section 04 are under 25%.
  • Housing stock, not traffic. Counts describe owner-occupied homes, not insurance shoppers or ad inventory. Delivery by ZIP code is confirmed from campaign data after launch.
